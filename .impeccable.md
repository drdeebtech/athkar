# Design Context — أذكار (athkar.site)

Source: the site owner, in conversation (2026-10-01/02).

## Audience
Muslims of all ages reading adhkar in every situation of their day ("يسهل على المسلم يقرأ الأذكار بسهولة في كل موقف"). Arabic only. Mostly on phones, often one-handed: after prayer, before sleep, while travelling, in distress.

## Jobs
1. Find the right zekr for the current situation in seconds (grid, search, quick links).
2. Read long Arabic text comfortably (size, bold, diacritics, night mode).
3. Count repetitions without losing place (tap counter, progress, auto-advance).
4. Share a zekr (copy, WhatsApp) — "الدال على الخير كفاعله".

## Personality
Calm, warm, gentle, trustworthy, quietly joyful. Never flashy, never "tech". Reverent toward the text: decoration frames the words, never competes with them.

## Requested style
Claymorphism ("use claymorphism ui style") with the best possible colour palette ("make the website look amazing").

## Direction: "صندوق الصلصال" (a box of soft clay)
- Puffy, rounded clay surfaces: soft drop shadow + inner top highlight + inner bottom shade. Light comes from above (direction-neutral for RTL).
- Warm sand base, sage-green primary, apricot accent for the counter. Each section has its own glaze hue from one OKLCH formula.
- Inputs and wells are pressed into the clay (inset); buttons squish on press.
- Signature: original clay prayer-bead (misbaha) motif, echoing counting.
- Fonts: Baloo Bhaijaan 2 (rounded Arabic display), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (UI), Amiri (adhkar text).
- Night mode: "clay at dusk", deep sage-slate with soft highlights, no glow.
